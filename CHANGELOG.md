# Tvaro IPTV Player changelog

## 0.1.26 — Android — 2026-11-10

### New

- Control playback from headphones, Google Assistant and your phone's media controls

### Improved

- Large libraries load faster, use less data and refresh in the background without interrupting you
- Home, Movies, Series and the TV Guide respond faster on Android TV and Fire TV

### Fixed

- The mini player always shows the whole picture and is easier to reach with the remote
- Bug fixes and performance improvements

## 0.1.25 — Desktop — 2026-11-10

### Improved

- Large libraries load faster, use less data and refresh in the background without interrupting you
- Home shows the picture for a title as soon as you pick it, and changing channels starts right away

### Fixed

- The mini player always shows the whole picture
- Bug fixes and performance improvements

## 0.1.31 — Samsung TV — 2026-11-10

### Fixed

- Opens quickly and runs smoothly with very large libraries
- Sending a problem report no longer closes the app
- Bug fixes and performance improvements

### Fixed

## 0.1.18 — TV Manager — 2026-11-10

### Improved

- Your TVs stay in the list even when they're off, and you can update a saved TV in one click without searching again
- Shows when a TV is off and when an update is ready, as soon as you open the app
- Finds your Samsung TV faster on computers with virtual network adapters

### Fixed

- Bug fixes and performance improvements

## 0.1.20 — TV Manager for Android — 2026-11-10

### Fixed

- TV Manager installs on Android phones again without being blocked
- Android TVs show a simple way to install: the Downloader app on the TV, or TV Manager on a computer
