# Tvaro IPTV Player changelog

## 0.1.26 — Android — 2026-11-10

### New

- Control playback from headphones, Google Assistant and your phone's media controls

### Improved

- Large libraries load faster, use less data and refresh in the background without interrupting you
- Home, Movies, Series and the TV Guide respond faster on Android TV and Fire TV

### Fixed

- The mini player always shows the whole picture and is easier to reach with the remote
- Bug fixes and performance improvements

## 0.1.24 — Desktop — 2026-11-09

### New

- Tvaro IPTV Player is now available as an Apple-verified app for Mac

### Fixed

- Bug fixes and performance improvements

## 0.1.31 — Samsung TV — 2026-11-10

### Fixed

- Opens quickly and runs smoothly with very large libraries
- Sending a problem report no longer closes the app
- Bug fixes and performance improvements

## 0.1.30 — LG TV — 2026-11-10

### Improved

- Home, Movies, Series and the TV Guide open faster and stay smooth as you move around, even with very large libraries
- The Home picture changes the moment you pick a title, and changing channels starts right away

### Fixed

- The mini player and the guide preview show the picture again, always in full, and are easier to reach with the remote
- More channels play that used to stop with an error
- Bug fixes and performance improvements

## 0.1.17 — TV Manager — 2026-11-09

### New

- Tvaro TV Manager is now available as an Apple-verified app for Mac

### Fixed

- Bug fixes and performance improvements

## 0.1.19 — TV Manager for Android — 2026-11-10

### Improved

- Your TVs stay in the list even when they're off, and you can update a saved TV in one tap without searching again
- Tapping an update notification takes you straight to the TV that needs it

### Fixed

- Bug fixes and performance improvements
