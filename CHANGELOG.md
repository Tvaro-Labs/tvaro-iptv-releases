# Tvaro IPTV Player changelog

## 0.1.16 — Android — 2026-11-02

### New

- Sign in on your TV from your phone: scan the QR code on the TV, then send a saved account or enter your details in the app

### Improved

- Sharper app icons

## 0.1.15 — Desktop — 2026-11-01

### New

- If your version is too old to keep working, the app now tells you clearly and shows how to update

### Improved

- Faster startup
- Stronger protection for updates and downloads
- Better overall stability on every platform

## 0.1.17 — TV — 2026-11-03

### Improved

- Sharper Tvaro logo

### Fixed

- If the app can't start, it now shows a message with options to restart or reset instead of staying on the loading screen

## 0.1.6 — TV Installer — 2026-11-03

### Improved

- A cleaner home screen, centred in the window
- A shorter Help link with its own icon at the bottom of the window

### Fixed

- Help and about no longer shows two scrollbars

## 0.1.6 — TV Installer for Android — 2026-11-03

### Fixed

- The screen you see while the app opens now matches your light or dark theme
