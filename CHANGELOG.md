# Tvaro IPTV Player changelog

## 0.1.17 — Android — 2026-11-04

### New

- Choose what you share when you first open the app

### Improved

- A sharper Tvaro logo and app icons

### Fixed

- If the app can't start, it now shows a message with options to restart or reset instead of staying on the loading screen
- Pinching or double-tapping no longer zooms the app

## 0.1.17 — Desktop — 2026-11-04

### New

- Choose what you share when you first open the app

## 0.1.17 — TV — 2026-11-03

### Improved

- Sharper Tvaro logo

### Fixed

- If the app can't start, it now shows a message with options to restart or reset instead of staying on the loading screen

## 0.1.8 — TV Manager — 2026-11-04

### New

- Tvaro TV Installer is now Tvaro TV Manager, with a new logo. Your TVs, sign-ins and settings stay as they are, and updates keep working
- Choose which copy to remove when a Samsung TV has more than one

### Improved

- Uninstalling from Samsung TVs works on more TVs, and tells you if there's an icon left to delete

## 0.1.7 — TV Installer for Android — 2026-11-04

### New

- Uninstall Tvaro IPTV Player from your TV right in the app
- Fix an install that's blocked by a copy from another device or account
- Choose what you share when you first open the app

### Fixed

- Pinching or double-tapping no longer zooms the app
