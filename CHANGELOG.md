# Tvaro IPTV changelog

## 0.1.6 — 2026-10-31

### New

- Many more videos play on Windows, including MKV, HEVC and Dolby/DTS audio, with hardware decoding
- Video previews: focus a movie, series or channel and a short muted clip plays from the middle; turn it off in Settings › Player
- The home hero plays a preview of the featured title
- Resize the mini player by dragging its corner, move it to any corner, and double-click for a larger size
- Multiview layouts: grid, one large with three small, side by side and one plus two, with adjustable dividers and tile swapping

### Improved

- Clearer messages that say what went wrong and what to do next
- When your provider limits connections, Tvaro tells you and retries gently instead of repeatedly
- User-Agent and Referer settings for an account now apply on desktop
- Smoother seeking: the picture holds while skipping and quick skips combine into one jump
- Playback keeps going when you minimize the window
- The number keys on the numpad change channels
- A simpler Diagnostics screen with a short privacy note

### Fixed

- Live TV and the channel list no longer close with an error when a playlist lists the same channel twice
- The screen no longer shifts when a side panel opens
- Videos your provider refuses are no longer reported as an unsupported format

## 0.1.5 — 2026-10-30

### New

- A richer home screen: Top 10, Because you watched, Live sports, News channels, Best of the year and rows for your biggest categories
- Play and More info buttons on the home screen on desktop
- A redesigned Accounts screen that shows expiry, connections and the last update at a glance
- Midnight is the new default theme, with a matching app icon

### Improved

- Type straight into text fields on desktop, with a normal cursor, selection and copy and paste
- The home screen mixes movies and series instead of showing mostly series
- Artwork loads faster and uses less memory on desktop
- Names that mix Arabic and English stay readable when they're shortened
- Arabic genres are separated with the Arabic comma
- Icons look the same everywhere in the app
- Dates follow the app's language
- Tvaro checks for updates every hour and when you come back to it
- Thin, dark scrollbars

### Fixed

- The mouse wheel scrolls what's under the cursor on Home and in Settings instead of moving the side menu
- Adding an account whose server port doesn't answer now connects without the port
- The What's new title is no longer cut off

## 0.1.4 — 2026-10-30

### New

- Mini player: keep watching in a corner window while you browse
- Multiview on desktop: watch up to four live channels at once and pick which one you hear
- Notifications: a bell in the top bar shows news and updates from Tvaro
- Account details: see your subscription status, expiry and connections in Settings › Accounts

### Improved

- Menus open over a soft blurred background on desktop
- Keyboard shortcuts on desktop now show the keys you actually press
- Escape closes menus and leaves full screen, and never closes the app
- Tab moves through the app without jumping to the window buttons
- The mouse wheel scrolls category lists without switching category
- Full screen from a maximized window now covers the whole screen
- Layout fits small windows without overlapping
- "Show channel" is now "Unhide channel"

### Fixed

- Double-clicking a channel no longer opens it and goes full screen at the same time
- Dragging from one card to another no longer opens the second card
- Watch in multiview from a channel's options now works

## 0.1.3 — 2026-10-30

### New

- A new title bar: window controls now blend into the app instead of the standard Windows bar
- Send logs to Tvaro from Settings › About › Diagnostics and get a support code to share with us

### Improved

- Lattice is now the default background pattern
- Text no longer gets highlighted by accident while you use the app

## 0.1.2 — 2026-10-30

### New

- Tvaro IPTV has a new name and app icon
- A new setup wizard in English and Arabic

### Improved

- Cards, panels and placeholders now follow your theme colour
- The window reopens maximized if you left it maximized
- More room under Arabic titles on the home screen

### Fixed

- Changing the theme on one profile no longer changes it on other profiles

## 0.1.1 — 2026-10-30

- Redesigned player options: one clean list with a check on the current choice
- Lower memory use during playback and while the window is minimized
- Large playlists and TV guides load with far less memory

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-30

- First desktop release for Windows
