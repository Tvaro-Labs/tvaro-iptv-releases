# Tvaro IPTV Player changelog

## 0.1.16 — Android — 2026-11-02

### New

- Sign in on your TV from your phone: scan the QR code on the TV, then send a saved account or enter your details in the app

### Improved

- Sharper app icons

## 0.1.15 — Desktop — 2026-11-01

### New

- If your version is too old to keep working, the app now tells you clearly and shows how to update

### Improved

- Faster startup
- Stronger protection for updates and downloads
- Better overall stability on every platform

## 0.1.16 — TV — 2026-11-02

### New

- Sign in with your phone: scan the QR code on your TV and enter your details there instead of typing with the remote

### Improved

- Sharper app icons

## 0.1.5 — TV Installer — 2026-11-03

### New

- A new Settings screen with categories for General, Updates, Accounts, Privacy, and Help and about
- Sign in with more than one Samsung account and see which TVs use each one
- Install a saved copy of Tvaro IPTV Player on your TV when there's no internet
- A QR code and download button make it easy to get the Android app from your computer
- On Android phones, choose which update notifications you get right in the app
- Privacy policy and Terms of use links in Help and about

### Improved

- A sharper, cleaner Tvaro logo on app icons and screens
- Android phones now get a simpler top bar, full-screen language and theme choices, and bottom sheets
- Clear messages when your Wi-Fi or internet connection is down, and installs pick up again when it's back
- When a TV already has Tvaro IPTV Player from another device or account, the app explains it, shows your account and offers a way forward
- A dropdown for choosing your language, ready for more languages

### Fixed

- On Android 17, finding TVs and adding one by IP address work again once you allow Nearby devices
- A saved copy of Tvaro IPTV Player no longer replaces a newer version on your TV
- On Android phones, the System theme now applies right away
- Signing in again after you cancel or close the browser now works
- Settings rows no longer sit under the navigation bar on Android phones
- The theme choice no longer cuts off "System"
- Messages no longer cover the screen title on Android phones
